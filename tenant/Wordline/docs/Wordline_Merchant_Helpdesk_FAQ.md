# Wordline Merchant Helpdesk — Paper Roll and Battery FAQ

This guide covers the two topics the Wordline merchant helpdesk voice assistant
supports for merchants calling about their card-payment terminal (POS
machine): paper roll requests and terminal battery problems. The facts below
are the same facts the assistant's guided flow speaks.

## Verifying the terminal

**What does the helpdesk need before helping me?**
Your Terminal ID (TID), the 7 or 8 character identifier of the POS terminal.
The helpdesk looks the Terminal ID up in the merchant records and reads back
the outlet name, bank name and terminal model (and, for battery problems, the
registered contact number) for you to confirm.

**What if my Terminal ID is not found?**
The helpdesk asks you to repeat the Terminal ID slowly, one digit at a time.
If it still cannot be found, the helpdesk continues to help with the paper
roll request or the battery troubleshooting without the verified details.

## Paper roll request by SMS

**How do I request paper rolls by SMS?**
Send an SMS in this format: ROLL, a space, your MID number with a zero added
before it, a space, and the required quantity. Example: ROLL 0<MID> 5. Send
the SMS to 575758. Double-check the MID number and the quantity before
sending.

**What is the SMS number for paper rolls?**
575758.

## Paper roll request by IVR

**How do I request paper rolls through the IVR?**
Step one: call 1860 233 2332, or call 022 4042 6060. Step two: select your
preferred language. Step three: enter your Terminal ID. Step four: press 2 to
select the paper roll request option. Step five: select the required quantity
by pressing 1 to 9. Step six: enter any valid ten-digit mobile number and
press hash. A docket number is sent by SMS to the mobile number you entered.

**What are the IVR phone numbers?**
1860 233 2332 and 022 4042 6060.

## Paper roll request raised by the helpdesk

**Can the helpdesk raise the paper roll request for me?**
Yes. If you are unable to use SMS or the IVR, the helpdesk raises the request
on your behalf. It needs: your merchant or outlet name, your TID number, your
complete address, your work order details including your weekly off, your
preferred working time for the team's visit, your contact number, the contact
person's name, and the quantity.

**How many paper rolls can I request?**
Between one and nine rolls per request.

## Battery problem — battery looks fine

**My terminal battery drains quickly. What should I check first?**
Switch the terminal off, open the back panel and check whether the battery is
overheating, swollen or showing any visible damage. If the battery looks fine:
remove it, clean the battery contacts with a dry soft cloth, reconnect it,
close the back panel and switch the terminal on.

**How should I charge the terminal after cleaning the contacts?**
Make sure the charging adapter is connected to the power socket and the
terminal and that the charging indicator shows on the terminal. If an
alternate compatible adapter is available, you may try it to rule out the
adapter. Keep the terminal on charge for at least 4 hours.

**What if the battery is still below 80% after 4 hours of charging?**
Call the helpdesk again. A service docket is raised for a Field Service
Engineer (FSE) visit to inspect and resolve the issue.

## Battery problem — battery damaged

**The battery is hot, swollen, leaking or smoking. What should I do?**
For your safety, do not remove, reconnect or charge the damaged battery and do
not continue using the terminal; keep it switched off. The helpdesk raises a
service docket and arranges a field engineer visit for battery replacement.

**What details are needed for the engineer visit?**
Merchant or outlet name, TID, complete address, weekly off details, preferred
working time, contact number and the contact person's name.

## Service docket

**What is a service docket number?**
The reference number of the service request raised for a Field Service
Engineer visit. Note it down for future reference. The engineer visits as per
the service arrangement and work order process.

## Scope

**What can the helpdesk assistant help with?**
Paper roll requests (SMS, IVR, or raised on your behalf) and terminal battery
problems, including a battery that stays below 80% after charging. For other
terminal matters, the helpdesk team assists further.
