# UPI payment failure while subscribing to ZEE5

Source: https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/upi-payment-failed-while-subscribing-to-zee-5-here-s-how-to-fix-it

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

First check the UPI app's transaction history.

- If there is no debit and the transaction says failed, no money was deducted. The customer can retry after addressing the failure.
- If money was debited but the plan is inactive, do not pay again immediately. Check the signed-in account, confirmation email or SMS, and allow time for reconciliation.

Documented common causes include an incorrect UPI PIN or an expired payment window, a bank or UPI service outage, the bank's daily transaction limit, insufficient balance, temporary UPI disablement after SIM or app changes, and weak internet connectivity.

For a debited but inactive transaction, ZEE5's UPI article advises checking confirmation messages and contacting support with the UPI reference or UTR, payment date and time, amount, and a screenshot if the plan remains inactive. The article says failed debited UPI payments are typically auto-refunded by the bank within three to five business days if activation does not occur. This is general guidance, not a verified refund promise for an individual transaction.

The bot must never ask the customer to speak a UPI PIN, card number, CVV, password, or OTP.
