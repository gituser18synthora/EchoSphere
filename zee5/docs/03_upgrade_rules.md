# ZEE5 upgrade rules and steps

Source section: Upgrading a subscription

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

## Documented upgrade paths

For a customer already on a monthly subscription, ZEE5 documents upgrading through the **Upgrade** button by selecting a Yearly All Access plan and continuing to the payment page.

For a customer on a monthly Regional Language subscription, ZEE5 documents these target choices:

- a yearly plan in the same language category;
- an All Access plan; or
- an All Access + Sports plan.

The Help Center does not establish every possible source-plan and target-plan combination. For any path not explicitly documented, the customer must use the Upgrade screen on the signed-in account to see whether ZEE5 offers that path. The bot must not declare an undocumented path eligible.

If the customer has no active subscription, this is a new purchase rather than an upgrade. Guide the customer through the purchase flow.

## Steps

1. Sign in to the ZEE5 account that holds the active subscription.
2. Select **Upgrade**.
3. Choose an upgrade option shown to that account.
4. Continue to the payment page.
5. Review the final differential amount shown by ZEE5 and complete payment there if desired.

The bot cannot inspect an account, determine account-specific eligibility, take payment, or confirm activation without a ZEE5 backend integration.

## Differential amount and validity

The Subscription page may display the full price of the target plan. ZEE5 states that the payment page displays the differential amount actually being charged for the upgrade. The unused value of the current subscription is adjusted against the new plan. The final amount can vary with the current plan, remaining validity, taxes, and promotions.

Never calculate the differential amount. Never quote an illustrative example as the customer's payable amount. After an upgrade, validity is adjusted according to the upgraded pack and the number of days already used in the current subscription.
