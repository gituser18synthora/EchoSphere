# Payment completed but ZEE5 subscription is inactive or missing

Sources:

- https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/i-made-a-payment-but-my-subscription-isn-t-active-my-subscription-is-missing-30-3-2020
- https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/paid-for-zee5-but-can-t-watch-yet-here-s-how-to-fix-it

Source section: Subscription issues before and during purchase

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

1. Update to the latest ZEE5 app version.
2. Confirm that the customer is signed in with the same email address, mobile number, or social login used for the purchase. If unsure, sign out and sign back in with the purchasing account.
3. A new purchase can take a few minutes to appear. Do not make another payment while the first payment may still be reconciling.
4. In ZEE5, open **Profile**, then **Control Center**, then **Plans & Rentals** or **My Subscriptions**, and check for an active plan.
5. Sign out and sign in again.

For an Apple App Store purchase, the purchase can be checked in App Store Purchase History. For Google Play, open Payments & Subscriptions and check Budget & History. If no platform purchase or confirmation exists, contact Apple Support or Google Support as applicable.

If the correct account still does not show the plan, contact ZEE5 support through **Write to Us** or support.in@zee5.com. Provide the purchased plan, payment date and time, amount, payment method, transaction or order ID, and a screenshot of the payment confirmation. ZEE5's newer issue article also requests the last four card digits if applicable and the registered email and mobile number.

The support bot must not claim that payment, refund, subscription status, or activation has been verified. It has no ZEE5 transaction lookup integration.
