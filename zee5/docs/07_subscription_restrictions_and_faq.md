## Country restrictions

ZEE5 subscription offerings are specific to the country where the subscription is purchased. ZEE5 states that the subscription does not work outside the country of purchase. To use ZEE5 in another country, a subscription must be purchased in that country.

## Free trials and offers

Free-trial availability changes over time and varies by country and platform. If an eligible free trial is currently offered, it appears on the ZEE5 home page or purchase surface. The bot must not promise a trial or promotion that is not shown by ZEE5 to the customer.

## Multiple subscriptions

ZEE5 permits multiple subscriptions, but advises that this is not beneficial because each purchase begins immediately and the validity periods overlap.

## Account and payment visibility

In Control Center, My Subscription shows the active plan, renewal status and dates, and billing information. My Transactions shows payment history, receipts, and transaction details. The bot can explain where to look but cannot see the customer's account.
