# How to purchase a ZEE5 Premium subscription

Source: https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-do-i-purchase-a-premium-subscription

Source section: Purchasing a subscription

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

ZEE5 offers country-specific subscription plans. The Subscription page is the authoritative place to see plans available to the signed-in customer.

## Mobile app

1. Open the ZEE5 mobile app and log in.
2. Tap **BUY PLAN** at the top left of the home page.
3. Select a suitable plan.
4. Continue to payment.
5. After payment, check the Order Summary page for transaction details. The support bot cannot treat a customer's spoken claim as confirmation of purchase or activation.

## Website on a mobile device

1. Visit https://www.zee5.com/ and log in.
2. Tap **BUY PLAN** at the top left.
3. Select a plan and continue to payment.
4. After payment, check the Order Summary page.

## Desktop or laptop website

1. Visit https://www.zee5.com/ and log in.
2. Click **BUY PLAN** at the top right.
3. Select a plan and continue to payment.
4. After payment, check the Order Summary page.

## Smart TV

1. Open ZEE5 on the Smart TV.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Select **My Plans**.
4. Select a suitable plan and continue to payment.

## From a Premium title trailer

Open a Premium movie, show, or series in the app or website. After the trailer, select **Proceed** in the subscription window, choose a plan, log in or register if prompted, and continue to payment. A customer who is already logged into an account with an active Premium subscription will not see the Proceed button on this trailer screen.

The bot can explain these steps but has no ZEE5 account or payment integration and cannot log in, charge, purchase, or activate a plan.
