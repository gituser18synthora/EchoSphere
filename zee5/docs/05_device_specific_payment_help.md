## Recent iTunes purchase missing in ZEE5

1. Update to the latest iOS ZEE5 app.
2. Open the ZEE5 mobile app.
3. Open the menu or Profile and Control Center, depending on the current app layout.
4. Open **My Subscriptions** or tap **Restore Purchases**.

Restore Purchases is available only in the ZEE5 iOS app, only for a recent ZEE5 Premium subscription purchased through iTunes or the Apple App Store, and not for ZEEPLEX rentals.

## Amazon Fire TV or Fire Stick purchase

ZEE5 states that Amazon in-app purchases made on Amazon Firestick or Fire TV can take up to 72 hours to be processed by Amazon.

## Control Center

The ZEE5 Control Center provides My Subscription for active-plan and renewal details, My Transactions for payment history and receipts, Restore Purchase on iOS, and Manage Devices for viewing and remotely signing out devices.
