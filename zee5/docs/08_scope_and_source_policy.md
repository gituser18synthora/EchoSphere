# ZEE5 subscription support source policy

Primary section pages:

- https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/zee1008/zee5-subscriptions
- https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/zee1008/zee5-subscriptions/purchasing-a-subscription
- https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/zee1008/zee5-subscriptions/upgrading-a-subscription

Main site: https://www.zee5.com/

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

For current plan selection, use the current Premium Subscription Plans article and the live ZEE5 Subscription or checkout page. For upgrades, use the current yearly All Access upgrade article and the differential-amount article.

Do not use the legacy 2022 article titled **Upgrade from existing Plan to Rs 1499 4K Plan** to calculate or promise a customer's current upgrade price. Its historical plan example conflicts with the current plan structure and predates the current upgrade guidance. Never perform a differential calculation; the signed-in payment page is authoritative.

Time-limited partner bundles and promotions are intentionally outside this KB unless the customer supplies a current official offer page. Their eligibility and benefits change frequently. Never infer offer eligibility from model memory.

When the official KB does not establish an answer, say that authoritative information is unavailable, direct the customer to the signed-in ZEE5 page or official ZEE5 support, and do not guess.
