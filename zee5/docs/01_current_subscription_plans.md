# ZEE5 current subscription plans (India, direct purchases)

Source: https://helpcenter.zee5.com/portal/en/kb/articles/premium-subscription-plans-12-5-2023

Source section: Purchasing a subscription

Reviewed: 2026-10-01

Scope: These prices and benefits apply to subscriptions purchased directly from ZEE5 in India. Prices, taxes, benefits, and promotional offers can change. The checkout page is authoritative at the time of purchase.

## Plan chooser

- All Access: all languages, KidZ, international content, and Live TV.
- All Access + Sports: everything in All Access plus live sports. Sports plans work only in India.
- Regional Language: movies, shows, and Live TV only in the selected language or language group. All Regional plans include ads.
- KidZ: content from the KidZ section only. Live TV is not included.
- Live TV: Live TV channels only; movies, shows, and other Premium content are not included.

All Access, All Access + Sports, Regional, and KidZ plans document UHD 2160p video and Dolby Atmos. UHD playback requires a 4K TV or connected device. Supported devices listed by ZEE5 are TV, Android, iOS, Fire TV Stick, laptop, PC, and tablet.
