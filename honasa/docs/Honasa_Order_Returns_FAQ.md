# Honasa Customer Care — Orders & Returns FAQ

This guide covers the two supported topics of the Honasa customer-care voice
assistant: order information and returns/replacements, for orders across
Honasa's brands (Mamaearth, The Derma Co, Aqualogica, Dr. Sheth's and BBlunt).

## Checking your order

**What do I need to check my order?**
Keep your order ID handy — it is in your order-confirmation SMS, WhatsApp
message and email. If you don't have the order ID, the mobile number
registered with the order works too; we will look up your most recent order
on that number.

**What order details can customer care share?**
The current order status, the expected delivery date, the courier handling
the shipment, the order amount and payment mode, any discount or cashback
applied to the order, and the refund status on that order.

**How do I track my order?**
Once an order is shipped, live tracking becomes available. Customer care can
send the tracking link over WhatsApp to your registered mobile number — tap
the link anytime to see the live courier status. Tracking is not available
while an order is still being processed.

**When does the expected delivery date appear?**
The expected delivery date is confirmed when the order is shipped. For orders
still being processed, the date may not be available yet; it is shared as
soon as the courier picks the order up.

**Where is my refund?**
Share your order ID or registered mobile number and customer care can check
the refund status on the order: whether a refund is in process, its amount,
when it was initiated and by when it should reach you. Refunds are credited
to the original payment method unless stated otherwise.

## Returns and replacements

**What is the return policy?**
Eligible products can generally be returned within 7 days of delivery,
subject to the applicable policy for the product category. Some categories —
for example personal-hygiene items — are not returnable. The system checks
each order's eligibility when you ask.

**How do I return a product?**
Call customer care with your order ID or registered mobile number. Once your
order is verified and eligible, a return request is raised and the return
link is shared over WhatsApp on your registered number — complete the return
by following that link.

**I received a damaged product.**
We're sorry! Share your order ID and tell us which product was damaged and
what the damage looks like. You can choose a replacement of the same product
or a return with a refund. A link to send the affected item back is shared
over WhatsApp.

**I received the wrong product.**
Share your order ID and tell us what you received versus what you ordered.
You can choose to get the correct product as a replacement, or return the
item for a refund.

**An item is missing or my order is incomplete.**
Share your order ID and tell us which item is missing. We can send the
missing item, or refund you for it — your choice.

**The product is defective or expired.**
Share your order ID and describe the issue. You can choose a replacement or
a return with a refund. Quality issues should be reported within 7 days of
delivery.

**How does the WhatsApp return link work?**
For every accepted return or replacement request, a link is sent over
WhatsApp to the mobile number registered with the order. Opening the link
lets you confirm pickup details and complete the return filing.

**What if my return is not eligible?**
If the return window has closed or the product category is not returnable,
the assistant will explain why and can connect you to a support executive to
review the case.
