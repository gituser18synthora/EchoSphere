# OYO Guest Booking FAQ

## What happens if the property is overbooked and cannot honor my booking?

If the property is overbooked, OYO arranges a shift to an alternate OYO
property nearby with an equal or better room at no extra cost to you. Our
support team confirms the alternate property with you before the move, and
your booking amount and inclusions carry over unchanged.

## How is my booking confirmed with the hotel?

After verifying your booking ID and the guest name, OYO can confirm your
booking directly with the property manager. If the property manager is not
reachable, our internal stock team validates the booking on your behalf and
we share the confirmation with you on the same call.

## What if my room is unavailable due to maintenance?

If your room is under maintenance, the property offers an alternate room of
the same or higher category at the same property. If no alternate room is
available, OYO offers a shift to a nearby OYO property at no extra cost.

## How do I get my booking voucher?

Your booking voucher is emailed to the email address registered on the
booking. If no email is on file, we collect your email address on the call
and send the voucher there. The voucher includes the booking ID, property
address, stay dates and payment summary.

## What if my booking shows as cancelled?

If you cancelled the booking yourself, nothing is pending and no charges
apply beyond the cancellation policy. If you did not cancel the booking,
the call is transferred to an OYO support specialist to investigate and
resolve the dispute.

## What are the standard check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early
check-in and late check-out are subject to room availability at the
property and may carry an additional charge.

## Will the price I booked at be honored at check-in?

Yes. The booked tariff is honored by the property. If a property raises a
pricing concern, OYO resolves it directly with the property — your payable
amount does not change from what your booking confirmation shows.

## Who do I contact for anything else?

For queries outside booking confirmation — refunds, complaints, new
bookings or modifications — the call is transferred to the OYO support
team from the same line.
