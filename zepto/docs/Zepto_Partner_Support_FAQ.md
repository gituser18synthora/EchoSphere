# Zepto Partner Support — Payout Deduction Concerns FAQ

This guide covers the four payout deduction concerns Zepto Support handles
on the partner support line, what information the support agent collects for
each concern, and what happens after a concern is registered.

## Which concerns does Zepto Support handle on this line?

Zepto Support handles four payout deduction concerns for delivery partners:

1. **MDND — Mark Delivered but Not Delivered**
2. **Raincoat, T-shirt and Bag related deduction**
3. **Onboarding Fee related deduction**
4. **RTO issue**

For any other topic — incentives, order assignment, app problems, accidents,
or insurance — the support executive team assists directly; the agent will
connect the partner to a support executive.

## What is MDND (Mark Delivered but Not Delivered)?

MDND means an order was marked **Delivered** in the app, but the customer
reported that it was **not delivered**, and the partner's payout was deducted
for that order. To review an MDND concern, Zepto Support collects:

- The deduction amount.
- The last 4 digits of the Order ID.
- The date or week the deduction was made.
- Whether the partner called the customer before attempting the delivery.
- Whether the partner reached the customer's location for the delivery.
- Whether the product was handed over to the customer, the security guard,
  or someone else.
- Whether the partner got a call from CX support regarding the delivery.

## What is a Raincoat, T-shirt and Bag related deduction?

This is a deduction from the partner's payout for the uniform and gear kit —
the bag, T-shirt, and raincoat. To review this concern, Zepto Support
collects:

- The deduction amount.
- How many times the deduction has been made.
- Whether the partner received the Bag, T-shirt, and Raincoat.
- The date or week the deduction was made.

## What is an Onboarding Fee related deduction?

This is a deduction from the partner's payout related to the onboarding or
joining fee. To review this concern, Zepto Support collects:

- The partner's date of joining.
- The deduction amount.
- The date or week the deduction was made.
- Whether the partner paid any amount at the time of joining.

## What is an RTO issue?

RTO means **Return To Origin** — an undelivered order that the partner brings
back and hands over to the store team. An RTO issue is a payout deduction
connected to such an order. To review an RTO concern, Zepto Support
collects:

- The deduction amount.
- The last 4 digits of the Order ID.
- The date or week the deduction was made.
- Whether the partner handed over the product to the store team, and if so,
  when the handover happened.

## What happens after I share my concern details?

Zepto Support registers the concern with the concern team. The team reviews
the deduction against the order and payout records and connects with the
partner — once a ticket reference is confirmed, the callback comes within 24
to 48 hours. The partner does not need to call again for the same concern
while the review is in progress.

## Will the deduction definitely be reversed?

The concern team decides the outcome only after reviewing the details. Zepto
Support never promises on the call that a deduction will be reversed,
refunded, or waived, and never states why a specific deduction happened
before the review is complete.

## What should I keep ready before raising a concern?

- The deduction amount and the date or week it was made.
- For order-linked concerns (MDND, RTO): the last 4 digits of the Order ID.
- For onboarding-fee concerns: your date of joining.

## Does Zepto Support ever ask for payment details?

No. Zepto Support never asks for card numbers, CVV, OTPs, PINs, UPI IDs, or
bank passwords on a support call, and never collects any payment. If anyone
asks for such details claiming to be Zepto, do not share them.
