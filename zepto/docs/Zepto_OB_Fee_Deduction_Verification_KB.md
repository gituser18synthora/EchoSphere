# Zepto Onboarding Fee (OB Fee) deduction — verification line KB

This knowledge base is the onboarding-fee section of Zepto's "OB Deduction
BOT" document, reproduced as given. It contains no policy beyond that document.

## What is the Onboarding Fee?

When a new rider joins the Zepto ecosystem, he or she is asked to pay an
Onboarding Fee.

The Onboarding Fee is different for different stores. The onboarding fee is
dynamic and can change at regular intervals based on requirements.

The Onboarding Fee can be paid at once, or it will be deducted from the
earnings of the Zepton along with the payout cycle, as per the applicable
Onboarding Fee.

## How can riders pay the OB fee?

Riders have two options:

- Pay the total amount in one go.
- Choose deduction in installments. The installment fee is visible in the
  admin panel as either "OB Fee" or "Standard Deduction".

## What is the upfront fee?

- Riders are asked to pay a minimum upfront amount at the time of onboarding.
- If the rider pays the entire amount at once, the upfront fee becomes part
  of the OB Fee.
- If the rider chooses installments, the remaining amount after the upfront
  fee is deducted in weekly payouts.

## ऑनबोर्डिंग फीस क्या होती है? (Onboarding fee kya hoti hai — वही जानकारी हिंदी/Hinglish में)

जब कोई नया rider Zepto join करता है, तो उससे onboarding fee (OB fee, ऑनबोर्डिंग
फीस) ली जाती है।

ऑनबोर्डिंग फीस अलग-अलग stores के लिए अलग हो सकती है (हर store की onboarding fee
same नहीं होती)। यह fee dynamic है और ज़रूरत के हिसाब से समय-समय पर change हो सकती
है।

यह fee एक साथ (एक बार में, one go) pay की जा सकती है, या फिर Zepton की earnings
से payout cycle के साथ deduct की जाती है — इसलिए यह deduction payout से कटती है।

## Rider onboarding fee कैसे pay कर सकता है? (OB fee kaise pay karein)

Rider के पास दो options हैं:

- पूरा amount एक बार में (एक साथ, one go) pay करना।
- Installments (किस्तों / किश्तों) में deduction का option लेना। Installment की fee
  admin panel में "OB Fee" या "Standard Deduction" के नाम से दिखती है — यही OB fee
  और standard deduction का मतलब है।

## Upfront fee क्या होती है? (अपफ्रंट फीस)

- Onboarding के time rider से एक minimum upfront amount लिया जाता है।
- अगर rider पूरा amount एक बार में pay कर देता है, तो upfront fee OB fee का हिस्सा
  बन जाती है।
- अगर rider installments चुनता है, तो upfront fee के बाद बाकी (remaining) amount
  weekly payouts से deduct होता है — बाकी amount हर हफ्ते के payout से कटता है।

## What does this call verify?

The rider has already raised a ticket about an onboarding-fee deduction from
their payout. Zepto Support calls the rider to verify the concern:

1. Was the onboarding-fee deduction explained during onboarding? If not, the
   agent explains the onboarding fee using the facts above.
2. Was the rider informed about the amount that would be deducted?
3. Is the amount deducted the same as the amount the rider was informed
   about?

The document's example confirms all three answers before saying the deduction
appears consistent with the onboarding information provided to the rider.
In this bot's continuation flow, that outcome requires a communicated amount
and a confirmed match, with no contradiction between known numeric amounts.
If the deduction was not explained during onboarding, the bot explains it on
this call and preserves that original answer. If the amount was not
communicated, or the deducted amount differs from the communicated amount,
the agent records exactly what the rider says on the ticket; the document
does not define a resolution for those cases, so none is promised on the
call. The agent then asks whether there is anything else about this
deduction the rider would like checked, notes it, and closes the call.

## Does Zepto Support ever ask for payment details?

No. Zepto Support never asks for card numbers, CVV, OTPs, PINs, UPI IDs or
bank passwords on a support call, and never collects any payment.
