# Zepto Raincoat, T-shirt & Bag Deduction Support — FAQ

This is Zepto's dedicated partner support line for the **Raincoat, T-shirt
and Bag related deduction** concern.

## What is a Raincoat, T-shirt and Bag related deduction?

This is a deduction from the partner's payout for the uniform and gear kit —
the bag, T-shirt, and raincoat.

## What information does this line collect?

- The deduction amount.
- How many times the deduction has been made.
- Whether the partner received the Bag, T-shirt, and Raincoat (yes/no).
- The date or week the deduction was made.

## What should I keep ready?

The deduction amount, how many times it was deducted, and the date or week
it was made.

## What happens after I share my concern details?

Zepto Support registers the concern with the concern team. The team reviews
the deduction against the order and payout records and connects with the
partner — once a ticket reference is confirmed, the callback comes within 24
to 48 hours. The partner does not need to call again for the same concern
while the review is in progress.

## Will the deduction definitely be reversed?

The concern team decides the outcome only after reviewing the details. Zepto
Support never promises on the call that a deduction will be reversed,
refunded, or waived, and never states why a specific deduction happened
before the review is complete.

## Does Zepto Support ever ask for payment details?

No. Zepto Support never asks for card numbers, CVV, OTPs, PINs, UPI IDs, or
bank passwords on a support call, and never collects any payment. If anyone
asks for such details claiming to be Zepto, do not share them.

## I have a different deduction concern

This support line is dedicated to one concern type. For any other deduction
concern or topic, the agent connects the partner to a support executive who
can help further.
