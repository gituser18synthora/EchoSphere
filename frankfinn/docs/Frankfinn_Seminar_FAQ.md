# Frankfinn Free Career Counselling Seminar — FAQ

Source: Frankfinn "Quality Call Flow" document and the reference counselling
call for the FIVT Ahmedabad campaign. This knowledge base answers student
questions about the free seminar, eligibility, courses and what to bring.

## About Frankfinn Institute

Frankfinn Institute of Air Hostess Training is the world's number one air
hostess training institute — it is known as the Harvard of air hostess
training. Frankfinn makes students job-ready through its certificate courses
for careers in aviation, hospitality, travel and customer service.

## Which career options does the seminar cover?

The seminar covers four high-paying industries:

- **Aviation** — airlines and aircraft roles such as ground staff, ticketing,
  cabin crew, air hostess and flight steward.
- **Hospitality** — five-star and seven-star hotels.
- **Travel and tourism.**
- **Customer service.**

Right now there is a huge boom in all four industries — lakhs of new jobs are
coming this year itself, so building a career here can be very rewarding.

## What is the free career counselling seminar?

It is a completely FREE career counselling seminar of about 45 minutes.
Industry experts and senior counsellors from all four industries attend.
They check each student's eligibility and explain the high-salary career
options in every industry, with live salary data. Seminar seats and
scholarship seats are limited.

## What is the seminar timing?

Entry is between 10:15 AM and 11:30 AM — the last entry is at 11:30 AM and
the seminar starts at 11:40 AM. Students are booked for the next available
day, usually tomorrow.

## Where is the seminar held?

At the Frankfinn Institute Ahmedabad centre: third floor, near Mocha Cafe,
C G Road, Ahmedabad. If you come by bus, get down at Gita Mandir bus stand
and take a rickshaw to C G Road. The exact address and your appointment
number are sent by SMS after booking.

## Who is eligible?

Passing 12th (higher secondary) is mandatory for the courses.

- 12th-pass students and undergraduates (graduation still in progress) are
  eligible for the **11-month certificate course**.
- Graduates, postgraduates and final-year graduation students are eligible
  for the **8-month certificate course**.
- A third-year student is asked whether it is their final year — final-year
  students take the 8-month course, otherwise the 11-month course.
- Students who have not yet passed 12th can attend after completing 12th.

Date of birth is asked during the call to confirm eligibility.

## What is the highest salary after training?

The highest salary offered to a Frankfinn student after training is
Rs 2,47,000 per month, working as cabin crew with an international airline.
Every student's outcome depends on their own skills — the seminar counsellors
show real placement records and live salary data.

## What about the scholarship?

Students who attend the seminar together with their parents can get an
exclusive scholarship of up to Rs 40,000. Scholarships are limited and given
on a first-come-first-serve basis; the exact amount is decided at the centre.

## Is there any fee? Do I pay anything on the call?

The seminar is completely free — there are no charges. Nothing is ever paid
on the phone call, and no payment details (card numbers, CVV, OTP, PIN or
UPI) are ever asked on a call. Course fee structure and scholarship options
are explained in detail by the senior counsellors at the seminar.

## What must I bring to the seminar?

- Your **Aadhaar card** — mandatory. Without Aadhaar there is no entry.
- If your parents accompany you, their Aadhaar cards too.
- Your **appointment number** from the confirmation SMS — entry is done with
  the appointment number.

## Can I cancel or transfer my seat?

Booked seminar seats are non-cancellable and non-transferable. Please confirm
a seat only when you are one hundred percent sure you will attend.

## How do I get my appointment details?

After booking, an SMS is sent to your number with the appointment number,
date, timing and the centre address. If the SMS does not arrive within
15 to 20 minutes, call the Frankfinn helpline.

## What is the Frankfinn helpline number?

The inbound helpline is 1800 258 7332. You can call it any time to ask about
your appointment, reschedule, or get directions to the centre.
